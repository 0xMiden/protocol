Added delayed execution to `AuthMultisigSmart`: `propose_transaction` records a proposal with a single approver signature, `cancel_transaction_proposal` cancels it for good with signatures over `AuthMultisigSmart::cancel_signing_message` from at least `cancel_threshold` approvers and no fewer than signed the proposal (a cancelled commitment can neither be re-proposed nor executed through the timelock), and the proposed transaction executes through the timelock once the chain reaches `reference_block + propose_expiration_delta + min_delay`, carrying an approval expiration in its `MultisigAuthArgs`. The auth procedure selects the execution path from the verified signatures, so a proposal only opens the delayed path and never blocks immediate execution.
Added `DelayedExecutionPolicy` (`min_delay` and `propose_expiration_delta` in blocks, `cancel_threshold`, the floor of the cancellation quorum, in signatures), updatable on chain via `update_delayed_execution_policy`.
Added `AuthMultisigSmart::update_signers_and_threshold_root`, `AuthMultisigSmart::update_delayed_execution_policy_root` and `AuthMultisigSmart::set_procedure_policy_root` so procedure policies can guard approver rotation, delay-policy updates and policy edits; `set_procedure_policy` rejects the delay-action procedures, whose policies would be ignored.
Added `StandardsDomainRegistry` with the `MultisigSmartCancelProposal` domain of the smart multisig cancel message.
[BREAKING] `AuthMultisigSmartConfig::new` and `Auth::MultisigSmart` now require a `DelayedExecutionPolicy`.
[BREAKING] `multisig_smart` accepts delay-only procedure policies again (`ProcedurePolicyExecutionMode::DelayOnly`), and `ProcedurePolicy::immediate_threshold` now returns an `Option`.

[BREAKING] `NoteConsumptionChecker` now rejects unconsumable `FEE_SPONSORSHIP` notes without executing them and drops a failing note together with its sponsorships, reporting a bundle whose sponsorship carries the wrong fee asset as `NoteFailure::Fee`; renamed `FailedNote::error` to `execution_error`.
Added `FeeSponsorshipNote::check_reclaim`, and `StandardNote::is_consumable` now decides a lone `FEE_SPONSORSHIP` note by its reclaim rules.
`MasmError` is now available without the `testing` feature, so code can recognize why a MASM procedure failed.

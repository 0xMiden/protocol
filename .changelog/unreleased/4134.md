[BREAKING] The multisig auth components now verify the approver signatures before paying the transaction fee and bind a fee cap into the transaction summary instead of the exact fee, estimated by the auth procedure with a margin (`MultisigAuthArgs::with_fee_margin_percent`) or set with `MultisigAuthArgs::with_max_fee`, rejecting a transaction whose fee and network note sponsorships exceed it.
[BREAKING] `fee::pay_fee` now takes the account's initial nonce and also returns the paid fee amount, and the new `fee::estimate_fee` computes both without creating notes.

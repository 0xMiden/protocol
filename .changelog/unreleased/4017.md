[BREAKING] `LocalTransactionProver::prove` now takes a `TransactionWitness` with the transaction inputs, final account header and expiration block number, and rejects a witness that does not match the execution.
Added `PartialAccount::add_asset_witness` and `PartialAccount::add_storage_map_witness`, and `PartialStorageMap`, `PartialVault`, `StorageMapWitness` and `AssetWitness` no longer track entries with empty values.

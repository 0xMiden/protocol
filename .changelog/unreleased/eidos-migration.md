[BREAKING] Updated to the Eidos-based Miden VM v1.0.0-rc.0 release, replacing Poseidon2, renaming the Falcon variant, and removing the Poseidon2 IES variants. Note builders now accept `rand::Rng` in place of `FeltRng`; seeded helpers use `EidosRandomCoin`. Routing parameters support EcGFp5+Eidos sealing keys. Hash-derived commitments, Falcon signatures, and IES ciphertexts must be regenerated.
